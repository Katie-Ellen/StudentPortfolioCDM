Music Video

Still Scrolling

I created a music video to the song Work by Charlotte Day Wilson. I directed and edited the video and used Premiere Pro for my editing. I worked with colouring and framing. When filming I was working on framing and setting up different shots that could lead into transitions.

Premiere Pro Filming

The process

Planning and shooting

For my idea I wanted to base it on struggling with keeping up with social trends and looks and how it affects the younger generation. I wanted to link it in with the “Connections” theme by showing how young people can lose their connections with the real world because of being on their phones all the time. I wanted to use as many shot types as I could and incorporate them into the storytelling of the music video. The song I chose is very slow and mellow and I thought it fit the theme well and there are enough sections to match the video up with to make it flow.

My role

Editing

While editing I didn’t want to add effects of transitions because I thought it would take away from the simplicity of the video and narrative. I used some shots more than once to use as flashbacks and my “dolly shot” as a rewind as she is reflecting on how she’s been ultimately leading to the ending. I cut up shots to stretch, add in flashbacks between, and cut out unwanted parts. With my cuts to different times and days I tried to make them like seamless transitions. I had a lot of close-up shots of her eyes to show how we always use them to look at our phones but not at what happens around us, the final shot her eyes liven up because she’s left her phone behind in contrast to the other close up of her eyes where she looks sad and we can see the reflection of her phone.

Reflection

Looking back

If I were to do this assignment again, I would try get more shots outside and play around with how they look. I would also try to use more dolly shots as I thought it was a fun shot to try and the overall look of them. I would also use more dutch angle shots to add a bit of dramatics to my video but as my video had a more sombre feel, I felt they would look somewhat out of place. I would also like to get better at creating more seamless transition shots without using any effects in editing. Overall, I think it was a very fun assignment to do and I enjoyed creating a story from a song and matching shots to reflect the feeling I wanted to portray.
